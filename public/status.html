The scale, and where 6 sits

  • 1–3Prototype. Cannot complete a BIA end to end.
  • 4–5Completes a BIA, but an expert must sit beside every stage and the output needs rework before anyone would trust it.
  • 6Completes end to end. Mechanical failures are caught, the output is presentable, a supervisor is still required for method judgement, and known correctness holes exist.
  • 7An unsupervised manager can run one BIA on one company and trust the result.
  • 8Several managers, several BIAs in parallel, no cross-contamination. Evidence quality enforced rather than assumed.
  • 9–10Several companies, real data, a third-party audit passes. Onboarding is a document, not a person.
